Privacy Policy

VideoSize · Effective October 21, 2026

Information we collect

VideoSize does not collect any personal information. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking tools, and the developer runs no servers.

How videos are handled

Videos you choose are converted on your iPhone and are never sent anywhere. Originals are never modified. While converting, the app keeps a temporary copy of the original in its own temporary folder and deletes it as soon as it is no longer needed. Results are shared or saved to Files or Photos only when you choose to. Location (GPS) and other metadata are not written to the results.

Permissions

Photos "Add Photos Only": used only to save the reduced videos to Photos as new videos. Videos are chosen with the iOS photo picker, so the app can access only the videos you pick; it does not request full library access.

Data stored on the device

The number of free conversions used (a single number) is stored in the iPhone keychain. If iCloud Keychain is on, Apple may sync it between your devices; the developer cannot receive or see it. The last chosen target size is stored in the app's settings on the device.

Payments

In-app purchases are processed by the Apple App Store. The developer does not receive payment information.
